# Milky Way Atlas

An interactive, hand-drawn survey of our galaxy — a single self-contained
`index.html` with no dependencies. Open it in any browser.

## What you can do

- **Pan & zoom** the full galactic plane (drag / scroll / pinch), from the
  105,000 ly disk down to the Sun's immediate stellar neighbourhood.
- **Click any marker** — nebulae, clusters, black holes, spiral arms,
  neighbouring galaxies — to open its survey card: plate illustration,
  key statistics, and field notes.
- **Enter the Solar System** from the Sun's card: an animated orrery with
  all eight planets, the Moon, Ceres & the asteroid belt, Pluto, the
  Kuiper Belt, and Voyager 1. Orbital speeds are proportional to reality,
  with pause / 1× / 10× / 100× time controls.
- **Read the night sky** — a star-chart scene of the celestial sphere as
  seen from Earth: 13 constellations drawn from real star positions
  (RA/Dec), with the Milky Way's band, deep-sky marks (M31, M42, M1, M57,
  the Pleiades), star names, mythology, and finding tips on every card.
- **Chase Halley's Comet** — a retrograde, properly Keplerian elliptical
  orbit through the Solar System scene, tail always pointing away from
  the Sun, with a follow camera.
- **Catch a meteor shower** — five radiants animated on the sky chart
  (Perseids, Orionids, Leonids, Geminids, Eta Aquariids) with peak dates,
  rates, and parent bodies — two of them shed by Halley's Comet itself.
- **Take the Grand Tour** — a guided 13-stop ride through the highlights
  of all three scenes, from Sagittarius A* to Voyager 1, with the survey
  card opening at every stop.
- **Check tonight's sky** — the chart computes the Sun's real position
  for today's date, draws it in place, and marks which constellations
  are lost in its glare; every sky card gains live "visible right now?"
  and "prime evening month" readouts.
- **Sit the Field Exam** — an 8-question quiz drawn from a 26-question
  bank covering the whole atlas, with explanations, a "visit the
  answer" jump, ranks, and a persistent personal best.
- **Atlas index** — a grouped list of all 59 objects with a "surveyed"
  progress tracker (persisted locally).
- **Surprise me** — flies you somewhere you haven't explored yet.

Positions are approximate and sizes are not to scale — it is drawn for
navigation and learning, not measurement.

## Run it

```
open index.html        # or just double-click it
```

No build step, no server, no network access required.
