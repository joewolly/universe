    Reading starlight

    The stellar alphabet

    Astronomers sort stars by surface temperature, hottest to coolest: O B A F G K M. Colour gives it away — blue-white runs tens of thousands of degrees, deep red barely four thousand. And hot means brief: the fiercer a star burns, the sooner it dies.

    Sizes drawn follow the main sequence; giants and supergiants (Arcturus, Betelgeuse) break the rule — cool, yet enormous. The classic mnemonic: Oh Be A Fine Girl/Guy, Kiss Me.
